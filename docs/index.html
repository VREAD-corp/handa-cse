Sample questionGeneral information

Under the common provisions on the Constitutional Commissions, within what period must each Commission decide a case submitted to it, and within what period may an aggrieved party bring the decision to the Supreme Court on certiorari?

Art. IX-A, Sec. 7: each Commission decides by a majority vote of all its Members 'within sixty days from the date of its submission for decision or resolution', and, unless otherwise provided by the Constitution or by law, its decision 'may be brought to the Supreme Court on certiorari by the aggrieved party within thirty days from receipt of a copy thereof.'

Source: 1987 Constitution, Art. IX-A, Sec. 7

What each level covers

Professional

150 test items · 3 hours 10 minutes

  • Verbal ability: word meaning, sentence completion, error recognition, sentence structure, paragraph organization, reading comprehension (English and Filipino)
  • Analytical ability: word analogy, symbolic logic and abstract reasoning, assumptions and conclusions, data interpretation
  • Numerical ability: basic operations, number sequence, word problems
  • General information: Philippine Constitution, R.A. 6713, peace and human rights, environment management and protection

What is in the free reviewer

The bank has 55 verbal, 45 numerical, 35 analytical, 30 clerical and 50 general-information items. Every general-information answer cites the article of the 1987 Constitution or the section of the law it comes from, with a link to the full text.

Practice mode shows the explanation right after you answer. "Balikan ang mga mali" brings back only the items you missed. The mock exam hides the answers until you submit, runs on the official clock, and gives you a score per part.

What it does not do: it has no actual CSE questions (nobody outside the CSC has them, and pages that sell "leaked" items are scams), and a good mock score is not a guarantee. The CSC computes a general rating and does not publish how raw scores convert, so treat our percentage as practice feedback.
