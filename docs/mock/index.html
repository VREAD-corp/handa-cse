Timed mock exam

Same length and clock as the real pen-and-paper test. Answers stay hidden until you submit, then you get a score per part and every explanation.

Professional150 items, 3 hours 10 minutes. The real booklet has 170 items: 150 test items plus 20 EDQ questions about yourself, which are not scored. We keep the full 3 hours 10 minutes.
Subprofessional145 items, 2 hours 40 minutes (real booklet: 165 items including 20 EDQ).
PartsIn booklet order: verbal, analytical (Professional) or clerical (Subprofessional), numerical, general information. The CSC does not publish how many items each part has, so our split follows the size of each part in our bank.
Rules we copyNo calculator, no phone. Use scratch paper. CSC Examination Advisory No. 10, s. 2026 (09 August 2026 CSE-PPT), part D
